What does location cost?

Take the same house — same size, age, rooms, lot, quality — and move it around the map. Its price changes a lot. Each area's color is that location premium: red means the identical house costs more there, blue less. 1.00 is the typical location.

The pandemic repricing

In 2018–19 the city traded at roughly an 18% discount to comparable county homes. By 2023 the discount was gone — the map you're watching is dissolving from the pre-pandemic factors to today's, and the biggest gains landed in the city's historically cheapest neighborhoods.

Housing looks cheaper the farther you go

The classic deal: monthly payment on the typical home falls from about … near downtown to … at … out.

…until you price the driving

Add one commuter's round trips (22 days a month, $0.70 a mile) and the distance discount shrinks — but survives: about … all-in at the far edge vs. … near downtown.

Two commuters: the discount inverts

With two commuters driving, the mid-ring (8–13 miles) runs … a month against … downtown. Moving out to save money stops working once both commutes are priced.

The bikeable zone

Inside about 3 miles a bike can replace the car commute entirely — an e-bike stretches that to about 6. The green dots are city-proper block groups: close-in, and cheaper all-in than the mid-ring once driving is priced.
